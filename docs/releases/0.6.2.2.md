# 0.6.2.2 — depot purchases, line assignment and action sounds

- Vehicle purchases can resolve depots whose visible geometry is offset from
  their construction origin, including the UEP catenary terminal. Lookup checks
  position, construction type and depot children, including cached entities.
- Automatic line assignment tries subsequent stops when the first is rejected.
  This also covers cloned vehicles. Explicit stop choices stay unchanged. Attempts
  use agreed simulation-step targets and stop after success or exhausting the line;
  newer vehicle orders cancel an older pending search.
- Successful local purchases/clones, sales, line assignments, replacements and
  depot orders restore the original confirmation sounds. Failed attempts and
  remote actions do not trigger these sounds; loading does not replay old sounds.

## Validation and known limitations

Read-only inspection in a running game confirmed a UEP depot missing from the old
six-metre query; the new lookup resolved that same depot. A controlled single-game
test confirmed a train on Holz 1 rejected stop 0, accepted stop 1 and left its depot.
The complete automatic fallback and purchase flow still need a multiplayer test.
Audio delivery has automated coverage but has not yet had an audible game test.

Regression tests cover offset/stale/wrong depots, host/peer stop targets, bounded
retries, clones, superseding orders, saved vehicle keys and cosmetic audio lifecycle.
The workflow validates the full MSI and verifies its uploaded download and hash
before publication. Different route topology or engine results between players
are not repaired by stop selection. Train-name desyncs and separate refund/balance
discrepancies remain open. Earlier 0.6.2/0.6.2.1 test limitations still apply.

All players must close their games normally and update to **0.6.2.2** through
**Mein Fork → Aktualisieren und spielen** in the existing launcher. Resynchronize
an already divergent world before testing. Check depot purchases, assignment and
cloning from a depot with an unreachable first stop, and confirmation sounds;
compare line assignments, departure and subsequent SYNC on all players.
